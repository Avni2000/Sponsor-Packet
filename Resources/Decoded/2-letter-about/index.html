About Us

Founded in October 2025, WiscoHumanoids is UW–Madison’s first community and competition team dedicated to designing and fabricating a humanoid robot from the ground up.

Our mission is to train the next generation of engineers and scientists by building a bipedal robot with dexterous hands and real-time decision making. Every part is produced in-house, from legs to brain.

Our vision: a robot that helps with everyday household tasks and improves quality of life for the elderly and people with disabilities.

300+ undergrad & grad students
10 technical subteams
31 degrees of freedom (Gen 1)
4′5″ tall · ~40 kg target
