What We Need

Building a humanoid entirely in-house takes more than funding. Cash, materials and equipment all go directly into the robot, so an in-kind gift is just as valuable to us.

Funding Turns our timeline into hardware
  • Finish both legs and begin real-world walking tests
  • Order our first power and battery boards
  • Build custom QDD joint actuators
  • GPU compute for RL and VLA training
Materials What the robot is made of
  • Actuators and BLDC motors
  • Battery cells for our 12S2P pack
  • PCB fabrication and electronic components
  • Aluminum stock, fasteners and 3D-printing filament
Equipment How we build and test it
  • Onboard compute (NVIDIA Jetson Thor, RK3588)
  • Sensors: encoders, IMUs, depth cameras
  • Test benches and bench power supplies
  • 3D printers and machining access
